# My projects

## 🎮 3D Scenery — FNAF 1 Reimagined

A 3D reimagining of the original **Five Nights at Freddy's** map,
turning its classic point-and-click environment into a fully walkable
and interactive 3D space.

### Highlights
- Environment modeled and textured from scratch.
- Custom props, furniture and environmental assets.
- Atmospheric lighting and scripted horror events.
- Faithful recreation of the original map layout.
- Fully walkable first-person experience.
- Interactive and ambient horror events.

**Tech:** `Unity` · `C#` · `3D Modeling` · `Texturing` · `Level Design` · `Lighting`

## 🧩 Puzzles — Baba Is You Level Design

A level design project inspired by **Baba Is You**, where we created a
sequence of original puzzle-platforming levels using the game's level
editor.

The levels follow the **Kishotenketsu** progression, gradually
introducing mechanics and increasing the complexity of the puzzles.

### Highlights
- Designed and implemented three original puzzle levels.
- Progressive difficulty based on the Kishotenketsu structure.
- Introduced and combined mechanics across multiple levels.
- Designed puzzles around gravity, movable objects, ladders and teleporters.
- Combined platforming and puzzle-solving elements.
- Inspired by *Donkey Kong (1981)* and *Sokoban*.

**Focus:** `Level Design` · `Puzzle Design` · `Game Design` · `Kishotenketsu` · `Baba Is You Editor`
